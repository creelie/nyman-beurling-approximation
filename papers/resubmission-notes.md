Version 3 has been retitled Exact Gram Inversion for Fractional-Part Approximation. It separates the linear, ordinary sawtooth and reciprocal Nyman–Beurling dictionaries and corrects the identities that depended on conflating them. It gives an explicit finite inverse and least-squares solution for the ordinary sawtooth dictionary, derives its asymptotic error constant and coefficient bounds, and supplies rational determinant and tail certificates for the reciprocal problem. The Mellin transforms, Hardy kernel, boundary evaluation statements, fixed-gain variance and scalar Riccati formula have been corrected, with complete derivations, new figures and appendices. Exact arithmetic checks and verified bibliography metadata accompany the sources. The Riemann hypothesis remains open; no finite computation or filtering estimate is presented as a proof of its limiting criterion. The published author list and corresponding author are unchanged.
